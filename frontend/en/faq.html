Solana Rent Reclaim FAQ

Everything about reclaiming rent, closing accounts, fees, and safety — answered in one place.

How much SOL can I reclaim per token account?

About 0.00203928 SOL — the standard rent for each token account (ATA) on Solana. The more dead coins you've traded, the more rent you can reclaim.

How do I check how much rent my wallet has locked?

Two ways: ① send your wallet address to the TG bot @solata_rent_bot for an instant result; ② open solata.top and connect your wallet.

Will it accidentally burn my valuable tokens?

Protected tokens SOL / USDT / USDC / PYUSD / USDS / EURC are never burned. Other token accounts with a balance will be burned and the rent reclaimed, so transfer out any valuable tokens first.

Can dead-coin or honeypot accounts (with tokens) be reclaimed?

Yes — that's our core advantage. Zero-balance accounts are closed directly; dead-coin and honeypot accounts (with unsellable tokens) are burned first, then closed to reclaim rent. Most tools on the market can only reclaim empty accounts — because dead coins and honeypots can't be sold, so those accounts can never be zeroed out.

What if my wallet has no SOL left?

Reclaiming needs a tiny amount of SOL for the on-chain fee (~0.000005 SOL per signature). Top up a little SOL first if your balance is zero.

Why do some accounts show "non-redeemable"?

Some projects set a closeAuthority (close permission) on their token, which makes the account impossible to close on-chain. All other accounts without a closeAuthority can be reclaimed.

How much does the platform charge?

0.0002 SOL per account — about 10% of the 0.002 SOL rent. You only pay when the reclaim succeeds; even 0 SOL accounts can be reclaimed.

Is it safe? Will my private key be taken?

Your private key never leaves your wallet. Every signature only "closes token accounts / reclaims rent" — it never sends out your SOL, USDT or other assets. The code is open sourced on GitHub.

What if I have many accounts?

Many accounts are automatically split into multiple transactions (~20 accounts each) and you'll confirm a few signatures in your wallet.

What is a Pump.fun volume deposit (accumulator)?

When you trade meme coins on pump.fun, pump.fun automatically creates a "volume accumulator" account for your wallet, locking ~0.0018 SOL, and some accounts hold accumulated cashback (sometimes several SOL). It lives under the pump.fun program — not a regular token account — so most tools can't see it. solata.top scans and reclaims it (deposit + cashback, 10% fee).

Why can't other tools see this deposit?

Because most tools only scan token accounts under the Token / Token-2022 programs, while the Pump.fun accumulator lives under pump.fun's own program — a second class of hidden assets. This is our technical moat: we reverse-engineered and support it.
